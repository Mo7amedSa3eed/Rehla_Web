Welcome to Rihla

How login works

Your account keeps your bookings, tickets, and wallet in one place. Use your email and password to sign in from any device.

Login details

  • Use the same email you registered with.
  • Password must include upper, lower, and a number.
  • After login, your profile and wallet load automatically.
  • You can edit your profile anytime from the profile page.

Why create an account?

  • Track bookings and ticket history.
  • Manage wallet balance and payments.
  • Access marketplace listings and resale flows.
  • Sync profile details across devices.
